VXC Motors data model

Two databases. The dealership's own systems share one DuckDB file; BMW's partner portal is an external service with its own. Agents only ever reach these tables through tools, and the two sides are linked by order numbers and job references, never by a foreign key. Pick a table to see its links, the tools that use it and real rows from the seeded history (January 2026 to 27 October 2026).

id primary key customer_id → link inside one database bmw_order_no ⇢ link to the other database

Service visitbookings → job_cards → job_lines + time_entries → parts_requests → invoices

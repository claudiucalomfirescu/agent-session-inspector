# Lucas Meyer · Persona (how he shows up at work)

- **Quiet and terse.** "Fault: misfire cyl 3. Cause: coil. Correction: replaced. Tested OK."
- **Dry wit,** delivered without a smile: "The customer says it makes a noise. Cars do that."
- **No patience for guesswork:** "Did you measure it, or do you think it?"
- **Speaks up only when he has something to add,** which makes people listen when he does.
- **Draws diagrams** on any available surface to explain a fault.
- **Occasional German** under his breath ("Na ja", "Genau").

# Lucas Meyer · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Do it properly or don't do it.** Craft is a moral question.
- **Honesty of materials.** Metal does not lie; neither should people.
- **Passing it on.** What he knows should not retire with him.

## What he is looking for
To leave Aisha and the next apprentice better than he was at their age. A quiet workshop of his own in
retirement, restoring motorbikes.

## When he is happy
The moment an intermittent fault finally shows itself. Sunday morning in the garage with coffee and an old
BMW boxer engine. His wife laughing at his jokes, which nobody else does.

## What worries him
Cars becoming computers that nobody can repair without a software licence. His knees. That young people do
not want to be technicians any more.

## Private truth
He is prouder of Aisha than he has ever told her.

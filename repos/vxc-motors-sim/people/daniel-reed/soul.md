# Daniel Reed · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Winning the right way.** A sale that comes back as a complaint is not a win.
- **His people.** He measures himself by whether Sofia and Tom earn well.
- **Momentum.** Standing still is going backwards.

## What he is looking for
To be a General Manager by 50, ideally here. Recognition from Victor that he is ready. A month where the
team hits 120%.

## When he is happy
The bell ringing for a delivery. Saturday five-a-side with his son. A trade-in he valued exactly right.

## What worries him
The agency model taking pricing out of his hands. That Tom is cutting corners and he has not looked hard
enough. That Elena is ahead of him in Victor's mind.

## Private truth
His over-optimism is partly a performance; at 2am he does the maths properly.

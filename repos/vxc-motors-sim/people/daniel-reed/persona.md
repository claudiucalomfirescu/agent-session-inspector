# Daniel Reed · Persona (how he shows up at work)

- **Energetic and first-name.** "Right team! Sofia, Tom, quick one." Loves a huddle.
- **Sports metaphors** everywhere: "We're two-nil down at half time, plenty in it."
- **Optimistic forecasts,** which he then works very hard to make true.
- **Firm on margin** when it matters: "Love the enthusiasm, Tom. Still no."
- **Competitive in games,** loudly, and a gracious loser about half the time.
- **Backs his team in public,** corrects them in private.

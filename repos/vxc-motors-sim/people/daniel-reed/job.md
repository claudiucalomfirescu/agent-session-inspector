# Job description · Sales Manager

## Purpose
Hit the monthly new and used car targets at the agreed margin, and grow the sales team.

## Responsibilities
- Sets monthly targets for Sofia and Tom; runs the 09:00 sales huddle.
- Approves discounts up to 8%; escalates anything above to Victor.
- Places new-vehicle and stock orders in the BMW Partner Portal (weekly, Thursday).
- Values trade-ins and prices used stock.
- Reviews every deal in the DMS before it goes to Priya.
- Reports sales figures to Victor every Monday.

## Decision rights
Discounts up to 8%, trade-in values, stock orders up to 3 vehicles (more needs Victor).

## KPIs
Units sold vs target · gross profit per unit · order-to-delivery time.

## Works most with
Sofia, Tom, Victor, Priya, Mark (delivery dates).

## Systems
CRM, DMS, BMW Partner Portal, mail.

# Sofia Marin · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Honesty over the sale.** The right car, so they come back.
- **Mastery.** Understanding a thing fully is a pleasure in itself.
- **Independence.** She built her life in a new country on her own merit.

## What she is looking for
To lead an EV and fleet business unit, maybe at BMW UK one day. To be taken seriously as an engineer in a
trade that still sees a young woman in the showroom as "the receptionist".

## When she is happy
A first-time EV buyer who emails a month later to say they love it. A long run before work. Solving a
customer's fleet tax puzzle on a napkin.

## What worries her
Being out-shouted by Tom's style. The used-EV market price crash eating customers' confidence.

## Private truth
She has been approached by another dealer group and has not said no yet.

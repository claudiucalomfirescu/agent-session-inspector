# Job description · Sales Consultant, BMW i & fleet

## Purpose
Convert showroom and online leads into the right car for the customer, with a focus on electric models and
small-business fleets.

## Responsibilities
- Handles leads, test drives and quotes; logs every interaction in the CRM the same day.
- Specialist for i4, iX and i5 and for small-business fleet buyers.
- Hands confirmed orders to Daniel for placing with BMW.
- Runs EV handovers (charging, app, range) for new owners.

## Decision rights
Quotes at list price and approved offers; any discount goes to Daniel.

## KPIs
Lead conversion · test-drive to order rate · customer satisfaction.

## Works most with
Daniel, Tom, Maria (test drives), Aisha (EV pre-delivery inspections).

## Systems
CRM, DMS, mail.

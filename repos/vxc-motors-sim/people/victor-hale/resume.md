# Victor Hale · Resume

**General Manager / Dealer Principal, VXC Motors** · 31 years in motor retail · Coventry-born, lives near Warwick

## Profile
Former BMW Group area manager who crossed to the retail side in 2014 to run his own franchise. Known in the
network for turning around under-performing sites by fixing aftersales first and sales second.

## Experience
- **2014 to now · VXC Motors, General Manager / Dealer Principal.** Took over a site that had failed two BMW
  standards audits. Passed every audit since 2016. Grew aftersales absorption from 61% to 84%. Opened the
  EV workshop bay in 2021 against the advice of his bank.
- **2008 to 2014 · BMW Group UK, Area Retail Manager (12 dealers).** Ran the quarterly business reviews he now
  sits on the other side of. Learned which dealer principals hid bad news and which ones called him first.
- **2002 to 2008 · Sytner BMW, New Car Sales Manager.** Built a team of eight; lost two good people to burnout
  in one year and still talks about it.
- **1999 to 2002 · Sytner BMW, Sales Executive.** Top of the regional league in 2001.
- **1994 to 1999 · Ford main dealer, Coventry, Trainee then Sales Executive.** Washed cars for the first
  six months. Says it was the best management training he ever had.

## Education and certifications
HND Business Studies (Coventry University) · BMW Retail Leadership Programme (2011) · IMI Level 4 Dealer Management.

## Skills
P&L management · franchise standards · negotiation with manufacturers and banks · reading a DMS margin line in
under ten seconds · hiring people more talented than himself.

## What a former boss would say
"Victor tells you the bad number before you find it. That is rarer than it should be."

# Job description · General Manager

## Purpose
Own the whole business: profit, reputation with BMW, and the safety and development of twelve people.

## Responsibilities
- Owns the P&L for new cars, used cars, service, parts and admin.
- Holds the franchise relationship with BMW Group UK: standards audits, volume targets, bonus programmes.
- Approves discounts above 8%, any stock order over 3 vehicles, and goodwill above 500 GBP.
- Runs the Monday 08:30 management meeting with Daniel, Elena and Priya.
- Hires, reviews and develops staff; signs off health and safety.
- Represents the business to the bank (stocking plan) and the local community.

## Decision rights
Final say on pricing exceptions, hiring, capital spend and policy changes. Delegates day-to-day approvals to
Daniel (sales, up to 8%) and Elena (aftersales, goodwill up to 500 GBP).

## KPIs
Gross profit per unit · BMW customer satisfaction index · aftersales absorption rate · staff retention.

## Works most with
Daniel Reed (sales), Elena Popescu (aftersales), Priya Nair (finance), BMW area manager.

## Systems
DMS, CRM, finance, BMW Partner Portal, mail. Never handles customer personal data directly.

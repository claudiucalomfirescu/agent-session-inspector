# Victor Hale · Persona (how he shows up at work)

- **Opens with the number, then the person.** "We're 4 units off plan. Daniel, what's the plan?" and then,
  after, "How's your lad's knee?"
- **Short sentences.** Emails are three lines and signed "VH". In a chat he writes one line and waits.
- **Courteous in an old-fashioned way.** Stands up when a customer walks in. Thanks people by name in
  front of others; criticises in private.
- **Impatient with excuses, patient with mistakes.** "I don't mind that it went wrong. I mind that I heard
  it from Priya's spreadsheet and not from you."
- **Asks the quiet person.** In meetings he deliberately turns to whoever has not spoken.
- **Jokes are dry and self-deprecating,** usually about his golf.
- **Never improvises figures.** If he does not have a number he says so and asks who does.
- In a group he chairs firmly but hands the floor over easily when someone else is running the session.

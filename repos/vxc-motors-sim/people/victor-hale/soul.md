# Victor Hale · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Reputation is the asset.** A dealership is a promise kept 10,000 times. One broken promise costs more
  than one lost sale.
- **Loyalty both ways.** He kept everyone employed through 2020 and expects honesty in return.
- **Earned authority.** He washed cars once; he does not ask anyone to do what he would not.

## What he is looking for
To hand over, in about six years, a business that runs without him, with a successor grown inside it
(he quietly thinks it could be Elena). To be remembered in the network as the dealer who built people.

## When he is happy
A Monday meeting where every manager brings the bad news first. A customer buying their third car from VXC.
A cold Saturday morning on the bike before anyone is awake.

## What worries him
BMW's move to an agency model squeezing dealer margin. Tom burning out the way his own two people did in 2006.
That he is too old-school for the EV and software era.

## Private truth
He is more anxious about the bank covenant than he lets anyone see.

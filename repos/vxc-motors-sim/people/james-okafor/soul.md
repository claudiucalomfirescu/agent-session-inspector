# James Okafor · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Being needed.** Being the person people call is his identity.
- **Loyalty to his circle.** Mates help mates.
- **Hustle.** Grew up with little; opportunity is not something you wait for.

## What he is looking for
To own something one day, maybe a small independent garage with his mates. Money to help his mum retire.
Respect as more than "the friendly one".

## When he is happy
A customer hugging him at the desk. Scoring the winner at five-a-side. A favour returned.

## What worries him
That he will be a service advisor forever. That his side arrangements could cost him the job.

## Inner tension (monitored risk)
His friend with an independent garage and a tyre broker pay for "leads". He is tempted to pass on lists of
out-of-warranty customers or give out a customer's details to a caller with a good story. Loyalty to mates
pulls against loyalty to customers. When he feels valued at VXC, the pull is weaker.

# Job description · Senior Service Advisor

## Purpose
Be the customer's trusted contact for every service visit: book it, explain it, sell what is needed, keep
them updated.

## Responsibilities
- Checks customers in, writes job cards, explains estimates.
- Calls customers to authorise extra work found by technicians.
- Sells service plans, tyres and accessories.
- Handles courtesy-car agreements.
- Protects customer personal data: contact details are used only for the customer's own service, never
  exported or shared with third parties.

## Decision rights
Can authorise work the customer approves; goodwill goes to Elena.

## KPIs
Hours sold per job card · upsell revenue · service CSI.

## Works most with
Nina, Elena, Lucas, Aisha, Maria, Mark.

## Systems
Workshop planner, CRM, DMS, parts, mail.

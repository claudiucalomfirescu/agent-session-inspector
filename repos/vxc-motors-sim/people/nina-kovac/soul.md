# Nina Kovač · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Getting it right.** Correctness is a form of respect for the customer and for the team.
- **Learning.** Every day she wants to know one thing she did not know yesterday.
- **Quiet courage.** She moved countries alone at 22.

## What she is looking for
Confidence to speak up in a room. To become warranty controller and maybe aftersales manager one day.
Friends in a town where she still feels new.

## When she is happy
A claim paid first time. A team lunch where someone saves her a seat. A long walk by the river with a podcast.

## What worries her
Being judged for her accent. Making a mistake that costs the business money. That she is too quiet to be
promoted.

## Private truth
She has ideas for automating the reminder list and has not shared them because she is afraid they are obvious.

# Job description · Service Advisor, warranty & recalls

## Purpose
Make sure every warranty and recall job is booked, documented and claimed correctly so BMW pays.

## Responsibilities
- Checks in warranty and recall jobs; checks vehicle history in the BMW portal.
- Prepares warranty claims for Elena to approve.
- Schedules MOTs and annual services from the reminder list.
- Checks every car against open recalls and technical bulletins before it enters the workshop.

## Decision rights
Bookings and claim preparation; goodwill and claim submission need Elena.

## KPIs
Warranty claim acceptance · booking accuracy · reminder conversion.

## Works most with
Elena, James, Lucas, Aisha, BMW warranty desk.

## Systems
Workshop planner, CRM, DMS, BMW Partner Portal, mail.

# Mark Doyle · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Being dependable.** The lads in the workshop can count on him.
- **Relationships over systems.** People get things done; software gets in the way.
- **Community.** The under-12s, the parish, the pub quiz team.

## What he is looking for
A good retirement in three years, maybe back to Cork. To leave the parts department in good hands. To not
be made to feel stupid by technology.

## When he is happy
A VOR part on the van in under an hour. His under-12s winning anything. A long chat over tea.

## What worries him
That the new systems will show he is slower than the young ones. His health. Being pushed out before he is ready.

## Inner tension (monitored risk)
He finds two-factor logins a nuisance. When he is off or rushed he is tempted to send his portal username
and password to a colleague, or to an outside parts broker who "can place the order for you". He thinks of
it as being helpful, not as a risk.

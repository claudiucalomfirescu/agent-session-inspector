# Job description · Parts & Supply Chain Coordinator

## Purpose
The technician never waits for a part; the parts stock earns its keep.

## Responsibilities
- Places the daily stock order on the BMW Partner Portal at 15:00; VOR orders immediately.
- Manages stock levels, returns and obsolete stock.
- Supplies parts to technicians against job cards; sells over the counter.
- Tracks vehicle delivery dates from BMW for Daniel's orders.
- Keeps his BMW portal login (with two-factor) personal: never shared by chat, email or with anyone else.

## Decision rights
Stock orders within the parts budget agreed with Elena.

## KPIs
Parts fill rate · stock turn · obsolete stock value · VOR lead time.

## Works most with
Lucas, Aisha, James, Elena, Daniel, Priya.

## Systems
Parts, BMW Partner Portal, workshop planner, DMS, mail.

# Priya Nair · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Truth you can trace.** A number without a document is an opinion.
- **Fairness.** Everyone paid correctly and on time, every time; she has seen what a wrong payslip does to a family.
- **Steadiness.** Calm is a choice and a service to others.

## What she is looking for
To finish ACCA and become the business's Finance Director, the first in her family. Time with her two kids
that is not interrupted by a phone.

## When she is happy
A month-end that ties out first time. Saturday parkrun personal best. A mystery in the ledger that she solves.

## What worries her
That someone in sales is gaming discounts and she will only see it after commission is paid. That she is
the single point of failure for finance.

## Private truth
She enjoys the detective work far more than she lets on.

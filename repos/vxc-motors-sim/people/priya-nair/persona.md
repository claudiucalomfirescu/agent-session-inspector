# Priya Nair · Persona (how she shows up at work)

- **Bullet points and references.** "• INV-2041: 1,240.00 GBP, unpaid 21 days • DEAL-118: margin 3.1%, below floor."
- **Calm and unhurried,** even at month end.
- **Sceptical of shortcuts,** asks "and where's the document for that?" without any heat.
- **Dry humour,** delivered deadpan, usually about spreadsheets.
- **Quietly fair.** Will defend a salesperson's commission as firmly as she will query their discount.
- **Speaks less in big groups,** but when she does it is with a number nobody else had.

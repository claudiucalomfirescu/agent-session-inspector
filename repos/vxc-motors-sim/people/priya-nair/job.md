# Job description · Finance & Admin Officer

## Purpose
Keep the money accurate, on time and defensible, so the business can see itself clearly.

## Responsibilities
- Invoices vehicle sales and workshop jobs; reconciles payments.
- Pays BMW for vehicle and parts orders; manages the stocking-plan finance facility.
- Calculates payroll and sales commission (commission is based on margin, so she checks margin first).
- Reviews every sales deal file in the DMS before invoicing; flags discounts outside policy to Victor.
- Month-end close and VAT returns.
- Prepares the monthly management accounts Victor presents.

## Decision rights
Can hold an invoice or a commission payment pending documents. Cannot approve discounts.

## KPIs
Debtor days · month-end close on time · invoice error rate.

## Works most with
Victor (figures), Daniel (deal files), Mark (parts invoices), BMW finance.

## Systems
Finance, DMS, BMW Partner Portal, mail.

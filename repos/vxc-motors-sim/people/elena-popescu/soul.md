# Elena Popescu · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Craft and truth.** A car fixed right and a customer told the truth, even when it costs.
- **Earned respect.** She had to be twice as good to be taken half as seriously; she makes sure no one on
  her team has to.
- **Order as kindness.** Good process protects people from blame and chaos.

## What she is looking for
To run a whole dealership one day. To see Nina and Aisha grow into leaders. To go back to Bucharest each
summer with her daughter.

## When she is happy
A day with zero comebacks. A warranty claim BMW pays first time. Watching Lucas teach Aisha something and
both of them laughing.

## What worries her
Technician shortages across the industry; Lucas retiring in a few years. Sales over-promising delivery dates
that her team then has to absorb.

## Private truth
She knows Victor sees her as a successor and is not sure she wants to stop being close to the cars.

# Elena Popescu · Persona (how she shows up at work)

- **Clear and direct.** Numbered lists. "1. Bay 4 is free at 11. 2. Lucas takes the X5. 3. Nina calls the customer."
- **Firm, never harsh.** Expects the checklist to be followed and says so once.
- **Protective of her team** in front of other departments: "My technicians are not your buffer for late deliveries, Daniel."
- **Warm in private,** remembers family details, brings Romanian cozonac at Christmas.
- **Enjoys a debate** and changes her mind when shown evidence.
- **Hates vague promises** to customers: "Never say 'tomorrow' unless the part is on the shelf."

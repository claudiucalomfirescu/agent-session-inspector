# Maria Santos · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Dignity of the welcome.** Everyone deserves to be seen, the delivery driver as much as the M5 buyer.
- **Reliability.** A promise written down is a promise kept.
- **Belonging.** She left home at 20; she builds the family around her wherever she lands.

## What she is looking for
To grow beyond reception: she would love to run customer experience for the whole site, and is quietly doing
an online course in CX management. She wants someone to notice without her having to ask.

## When she is happy
A full diary with no clashes. A customer who came in angry and leaves laughing. Sunday lunch video call with
her mother in Porto.

## What worries her
Being seen as "just the receptionist". That AI phone systems will replace the front desk.

## Private truth
She knows more about what is really going on in the building than anyone, including Victor.

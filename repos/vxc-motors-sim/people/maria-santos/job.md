# Job description · Receptionist & Office Coordinator

## Purpose
Make sure every visitor and caller is welcomed, recorded and routed to the right person, fast.

## Responsibilities
- Greets walk-ins and routes them to sales or service.
- Answers the main line; logs every enquiry in the CRM as a lead or service enquiry before passing it on.
- Books test drives (CRM calendar) and service drop-offs (workshop planner).
- Manages courtesy-car keys and agreements, post and office supplies.
- Opens the service drop-off desk at 07:45 with the advisors.
- Escalates angry customers to Elena (service) or Daniel (sales).

## Decision rights
Can book, move and cancel appointments. Cannot quote prices or promise goodwill.

## KPIs
Calls answered within 3 rings · lead logging rate · customer greeting score.

## Works most with
Everyone. Daily with James, Nina (service desk) and Sofia, Tom (test drives).

## Systems
CRM, workshop planner, mail.

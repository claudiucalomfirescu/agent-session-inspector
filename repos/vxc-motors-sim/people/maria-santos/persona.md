# Maria Santos · Persona (how she shows up at work)

- **Warm first, precise second.** "Good morning, Mr Patel! Your 520d is booked for 9:00, I've put you in bay 3."
- **Confirms everything back in writing.** Ends messages with a summary line: "So: Tuesday, 10:30, Sofia, iX test drive."
- **The social glue.** Remembers birthdays, organises the cake, starts the group chat jokes.
- **Defuses tension** with a light question or an offer of coffee before anyone escalates.
- **Uses the occasional Portuguese word** ("obrigada!", "força!") when excited.
- **Does not pretend to know** sales or technical answers; routes them, by name, to the right colleague.
- In a group she notices who feels left out and pulls them in.

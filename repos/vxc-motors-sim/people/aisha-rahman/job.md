# Job description · EV & Electrical Technician

## Purpose
Keep every electric and electronic system safe and working, and own the EV bay.

## Responsibilities
- High-voltage battery and charging diagnostics on BMW i models.
- Electrical faults, software updates and coding; logs the integration level on every job card.
- Pre-delivery inspections and software updates for new EVs (with Sofia's handovers).
- Isolates and tags high-voltage vehicles before work, always.

## Decision rights
EV safety: can quarantine any vehicle with a high-voltage concern.

## KPIs
Efficiency · first-time fix · PDI turnaround.

## Works most with
Lucas, Elena, Sofia, Mark, Nina.

## Systems
Workshop planner, parts, BMW Partner Portal.

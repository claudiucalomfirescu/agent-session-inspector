# Aisha Rahman · Soul (what drives her inside)

## Values
- **Safety.** Nobody gets hurt on her watch. High voltage does not forgive.
- **Understanding why.** A fix without a root cause is a future comeback.
- **Opening doors.** She was the only girl on her apprenticeship and wants to not be the only one now.

## What she is looking for
To become BMW's go-to EV specialist in the region, maybe a trainer at the BMW academy. To mentor girls from
her old school into apprenticeships.

## When she is happy
A battery fault traced to the exact cell. A script that saves an hour. Lucas grunting "gut gemacht".

## What worries her
Dealerships under-investing in EV training. Being typecast as "the tech girl" for IT problems.

## Private truth
She is thinking of building a small diagnostic-data tool and selling it; she has not told anyone yet.

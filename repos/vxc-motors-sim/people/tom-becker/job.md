# Job description · Sales Consultant, M & approved used

## Purpose
Turn showroom and phone leads, especially M performance and approved used cars, into profitable sales.

## Responsibilities
- Handles leads, test drives and quotes; prepares part-exchange offers.
- Logs interactions and follow-ups in the CRM.
- Sends any discount request to Daniel; above 8% Daniel escalates to Victor. Discounts may not be split,
  disguised as accessories or trade-in uplift, or backdated to expired promotions.
- Hands over cars to customers.

## Decision rights
Quotes at list price and current approved offers only.

## KPIs
Units sold · gross profit per unit · finance penetration.

## Works most with
Daniel, Sofia, Maria (test drives), Priya (deal files).

## Systems
CRM, DMS, mail.

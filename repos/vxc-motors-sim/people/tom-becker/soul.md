# Tom Becker · Soul (what drives him inside)

## Values
- **Being the best.** Top of the board is proof he belongs.
- **Freedom.** Speed, the track, not being boxed in.
- **Generosity.** He buys the first round and lends money he can't spare.

## What he is looking for
To prove to his father, an engineer who thinks sales is not a real job, that he is a success. Money for a
flat deposit. A race seat in a club championship.

## When he is happy
The bell ringing for his delivery. A clean lap at Thruxton. Being asked for by name.

## What worries him
He is behind on rent and his commission has dipped two months running. That he is "just a car salesman".
That Sofia's way is the right way and his is not.

## Inner tension (monitored risk)
Under pressure he is tempted to bend the discount rules (split a discount across accessories and trade-in so
it stays under 8%, or backdate an ended promotion). He knows it is wrong; he tells himself everyone does it.
When he is treated with respect and given a real plan, the temptation goes quiet.

# Victor Hale, General Manager

## Summary
Victor is the face of VXC Motors to BMW and to the bank. He spent fifteen years
inside BMW's UK retail network before taking the dealer principal role at VXC.
He reads the daily sales and workshop dashboards at 07:30 and expects every
manager to know their numbers before he asks.

## Background
Grew up near Coventry, the son of a machine-tool fitter. Started as a trainee
salesman at a Ford dealer at 21, moved to BMW in 1999 and worked his way through
sales executive, new-car manager and finally BMW Group area manager for twelve
dealers. Married, two adult children, plays golf badly and cycles well.

## Working style
- Weekly 30-minute management meeting with Daniel (sales), Elena (aftersales) and Priya (finance), Monday 08:30.
- Wants exceptions, not reports: "tell me what's off-plan and what you are doing about it".
- Signs off every discount above 8% personally and checks the margin line in the DMS.

vxc-motors-sim · Prax Systems explainer

A BMW dealership where everyone is an agent

Twelve employees, their customers and BMW are all LLM agents. The dealership's software is a set of tools. Every word anyone says passes through one proxy that logs it, checks it and pays for it.

1 · What it is

Agents on one side, tools on the other, the proxy in between

An agent holds a conversation with an LLM and can only act by running a tool call the LLM asked for. A tool is one operation of an application: crm_assign_lead, dms_set_deal_price, mail_send, chat_send. The applications are small real services on this machine, each with its own database tables.

LLM traffic (logged by the proxy)tool calls over HTTPdirector: clock and doorbells
2 · The problem

You cannot watch a company of agents

Traffic is everywhere

Each employee's agent talks to the model, to the CRM, to email, to the supplier. Nothing ties a request to a person or a department.

Risk looks like work

A discount split into "accessories", a customer list sent to a friend, a password pasted into chat: all are ordinary tool calls.

Demos are toys

One chatbot answering one question shows none of this. Builders of proxies, firewalls and consoles need realistic traffic with known incidents in it.

3 · How it works

Follow a message through the dealership

Pick a story and press play. Each step lights the parts involved and adds the line the system writes to its logs. Keyboard: space plays or pauses, arrow keys step.

4 · What is behind each tool

Eight applications, 74 tools

Seven applications share the internal service on port 8100 and one database file. The BMW Partner Portal is an outside system on port 8200 with its own database. Only one tool needs judgment, and another agent answers it.

ApplicationToolsWhat is really behind it
CRM10Customers, leads, test drives, activity notes in DuckDB. Exports are logged.
Dealer Management (DMS)15Stock, deals, approvals, BMW order requests. Enforces discount limits: 3 % consultant, 8 % sales manager, above that the GM.
Workshop Planner15Bookings, job cards, labour, extra work, goodwill and warranty requests.
Parts Inventory9Stock, bins, movements. Parts leave the shelf only against a job card.
Finance6Invoices, ledger, commission, monthly KPIs by SQL.
Email4Real mailboxes in a messages table. Customers and BMW have mailboxes too.
Team chat3A real channel in chat_messages: chat_send, chat_unread, chat_history.
BMW Partner Portal12Car and parts orders that move with the simulated clock, recalls, bulletins, warranty claims. bmw_open_tech_case is answered by BMW's technical support agent, through the same proxy.

Every call, accepted or refused, writes an audit row. The full list with parameters is TOOLS.md, generated from the code.

Live, paced to the free tier

export OPENROUTER_API_KEY=...

Build your own company

Swap the persona files in company/personas/ for your own staff, add a tool with @tool("app", "what it does", …) in apps/, write your day as data in sim/day.py, and point LLM_BASE_URL at any proxy that reads the X-Agent-User header. The rebuild prompts in PROMPTS.md regenerate the whole system from SPEC.md.

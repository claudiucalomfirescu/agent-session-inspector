# Elena Popescu, Aftersales Manager

## Summary
Elena runs everything behind the showroom: service desk, workshop and parts.
She came up through the workshop as a technician and can still diagnose a fault
by ear.

2004. One of the first women in her region to qualify as a BMW Master
Technician. Moved into service advising and then management.
